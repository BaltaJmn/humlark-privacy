Humlark

Whatever you hum, on the staff.

Humlark is an iPhone app for people who have a tune in their head and no way to write it down. Sing it, hum it, whistle it or play one line on an instrument, and the app engraves it as sheet music.

Get Humlark on the App Store

Everything happens on your phone. There is no account and no server of ours, and the app works in airplane mode.

What you do

Press the notehead on the home screen and make your melody. When you stop, Humlark finds the notes, works out the rhythm and lays out the staff with bars, ties and rests. You can play the result back, tap a wrong note to nudge it, and change the tempo, meter, clef or key. A voice memo you already have works too.

What leaves the app

Scores can be exported as a PDF to print, a MIDI file for GarageBand, Logic or any DAW, or MusicXML for MuseScore, Finale, Sibelius and Dorico. Exporting belongs to Humlark Pro, an optional subscription managed through your Apple account. Writing, playing and correcting scores does not need it.

What it cannot do

It follows one melody at a time. Chords, drums or several instruments playing at once are outside what it does.
