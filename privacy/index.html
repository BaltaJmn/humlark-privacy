Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Your voice and your scores never leave your iPhone. The one thing that does is an anonymous record of purchases, held by RevenueCat so the app can tell whether Humlark Pro is active.

Audio

Humlark asks for the microphone to hear the melody you hum, sing, whistle or play. It listens from the moment you press record until you press stop. The sound is turned into notes by code running on your phone; it is not uploaded, shared or used to train anything. If you import a voice memo or an audio file instead, it is read the same way, on the device.

Scores and recordings

Each score is saved on your iPhone with the recording it came from, so you can hear it again. We never receive them. Your own iCloud Backup may include app data, as it does for any app. Deleting a score in Humlark deletes its recording too, and deleting the app removes everything.

Files you export

When you export a PDF, MIDI or MusicXML file, you pick where it goes. Humlark does not send it anywhere on its own.

Purchases

Humlark Pro is sold by Apple. We never see your name, email or card; the app only asks the App Store whether a subscription is active.

To keep purchases in order across reinstalls, the app includes the purchase library of RevenueCat, Inc., which acts as our processor. It gives each installation a random anonymous ID and stores that ID together with the purchase history (product, date, price, transaction numbers) and basic technical details (system version, app version, storefront country). It receives nothing about your music. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. To have that record deleted, write to baltajmn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Humlark purchase data". Apple keeps its own order records under its own policy.

What is not in the app

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. RevenueCat is the only third-party code. Humlark works with the network off; the purchase record is sent when the phone is next online.

Children

Humlark is not aimed at children and collects no personal data from anyone. The only thing sent out is the anonymous purchase record described above.

Changes and contact

A new version of this policy will be published here with a new date. Questions go to baltajmn@gmail.com. The developer is Baltasar Jiménez Rincón, Spain (see the legal notice).
